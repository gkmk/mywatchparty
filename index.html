My Watch Party Watch multiple streams at once

All your streamers on one screen

No more 100 open tabs. Add Twitch, YouTube, Kick or Facebook streams and watch them side by side. The main stream plays audio, the rest stay muted until you click them.

How to watch multiple streams at once

  1. Copy the link of a live stream from Twitch, YouTube, Kick or Facebook.
  2. Click Add stream and paste the link. Repeat for every streamer you want to follow.
  3. The newest stream becomes the big main screen with sound. Click any small screen to switch to it.
  4. Use Copy share link to send the same multistream to your friends. Your streams are also remembered on this device.

Frequently asked questions

Which links work?
Twitch channels (twitch.tv/name) and past broadcasts (twitch.tv/videos/123), YouTube videos and live streams (youtube.com/watch?v=…, youtube.com/live/…, youtu.be/…), Kick channels (kick.com/name) and public Facebook videos.
Is it free? Do I need an account?
Yes, it's free and there's nothing to sign up for. Streams play through the official Twitch, YouTube, Kick and Facebook players.
How many streams can I watch?
As many as your connection can handle. Small Twitch screens switch to their lowest quality to save bandwidth.
Are there keyboard shortcuts?
+ adds a stream, 1–9 switch the main stream and M mutes or unmutes it. Shortcuts don't work while a video player has focus.
